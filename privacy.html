Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: JustWalk has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your step data stays on your phone. We never see it, sell it or share it.

This policy explains what information the JustWalk app ("JustWalk", "we", "us") uses and why. JustWalk is made by Ivan Betancourt.

Health data

With your permission, JustWalk reads your step count and walking and running distance from Apple Health. It uses them only to show your progress in the app: your daily goal, your journey and your weekly recaps.

  • Your health data is processed on your device and never leaves it.
  • JustWalk never writes anything to Apple Health.
  • Your health data is never sold, shared with anyone, or used for advertising or marketing.

You can turn off JustWalk's access at any time in the Health app, under Sharing > Apps > JustWalk.

Information you enter

Your daily goal, your choice of units, and any steps you add by hand are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, your Apple Account or your payment details.

To know whether you're subscribed, JustWalk uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. When you open the app or make a purchase, RevenueCat receives:

  • a random identifier created by the app, which isn't linked to who you are,
  • your App Store purchase and subscription history for JustWalk, and
  • basic technical information, such as your device model, iOS version, app version, and the IP address and country your request comes from.

RevenueCat uses this only to manage your subscription. No health data is ever sent to RevenueCat.

What we don't do

  • We don't use analytics or advertising services.
  • We don't track you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or rent any information.

Children

JustWalk isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

  • Turn off Health access at any time in the Health app.
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription in your iPhone's Settings, under your name > Subscriptions.
  • To have your subscription records at RevenueCat deleted, contact us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app.
